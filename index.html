How this demo works

What the AI does. You give it a story. Claude Sonnet 5 reads it, runs real web searches, and suggests people, records and perspectives the story could use. Our own code then throws out any suggestion the searches can't back up — and tells you how many it threw out.

What gets sent. Your text goes to the AI company, and its searches go out to the open web. This site keeps nothing — no database, no record of what you type. So don't paste anything confidential.

What it never does. It doesn't grade your story, it doesn't contact anyone, and it isn't an editor. Everything it suggests is a lead for you to check. It's told to suggest organizations, officials and public records — never private people.

Built for newsroom rules. Hearst's AI-use principles allow AI to help find and organize material as long as journalists review it and the AI use is disclosed. That's how this works: the AI finds leads, labels them, and a person decides.
